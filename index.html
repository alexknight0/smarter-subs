Learn the language while you do.

  • Review sentences containing words you don't understand

A dictionary that reads the sentence

Hover a word to pause the show and open its entry. The popup leads with the meaning that fits the sentence and shows the grammar patterns it's part of.

Still unsure? Ask AI explains the word in context. Right-click to mark it learning or known, or click to pin the popup. Esc closes it.

English only when you need it

Once you know every word in a sentence, its English blurs away so you read the Korean first. Press E or hover to check yourself, and allow a few unknown words per line if you like.

Fully customisable to fit your exact needs

Make it yours. Pick which language sits on top and how big each one is, choose the colours and underlines for unknown, learning and name words, and set how the English is hidden and how many unknown words a line may have. Every setting updates live.

Review sentences in the scene they came from

Sentences with words you're learning are saved as you watch. Each day the Review page suggests some to add, spread across those words.

A review replays the sentence in its scene, then asks what it means. Reveal the English and answer Understood? No or Yes; it comes back on Anki's schedule. Reviews run on a separate Netflix profile, so your watch progress stays put.

Track your vocabulary

A remote for the couch

Casting to your TV? Open the remote on your phone and sign in with the same Google account.

It shows the subtitles in time with the show. Tap a word for its dictionary entry and mark it known, unknown or learning. Big buttons play, pause, jump between lines, skip 10 seconds and change the volume.

Everywhere you watch

Sign in with Google and your marked words, word fixes, review sentences and settings sync between computers. Without signing in, everything stays on this computer.

Word analysis runs inside the extension with the Kiwi Korean morphological analyser, so subtitles aren't sent anywhere to be analysed.

Questions

What do I need?

Chrome (or another Chromium browser) on a desktop or laptop, and a Netflix subscription. Browser extensions can't run inside the Netflix apps on phones and TVs. To watch on a TV, cast the Chrome tab or plug the laptop in over HDMI and use the phone remote.

Does it work with every show?

It works with any title that has both Korean and English subtitles in your region. Netflix doesn't offer every subtitle language for every show.

Is it only for Korean?

The dictionary, word marking and reviews are built for Korean learners who read English. Showing two subtitle languages at once works for other language pairs too.

Does it change my Netflix account?

No. It adds the subtitles over Netflix's player in your browser. Reviews use a separate Netflix profile that you pick, so your watch progress isn't moved.

How much does it cost?

The extension is free. Ask AI is optional and uses your own Gemini API key.
